12 — FAQ

Outsourcing is
a big leap for your
development projects

On your path to greater velocity and flexibility, we’ve got you covered every step of the way.

What do you need to provide the project estimation?

We care about preparing well-rounded estimations based on the real product requirements. That’s why we always dive deep into your business context, specifications, and environment before we get down to costs. Usually, we kick off cooperation with a discovery session to understand your product and the scope of work. Then, any materials describing the project in greater detail are welcome, including a description of the project, user’s personas, story mapping, wireframes, and the like.

How soon can you start a project?

It depends on several factors such as our current capacity, particular product’s needs, or your responsiveness. Our experience shows that we can kick off the project within 1 or 2 weeks from receiving an inquiry. Having 250+ team members on board allows us to form and scale the team quickly while ensuring a short lead time.

What are your pricing models?

We offer a range of pricing models to keep flexibility in cooperation with our clients and adjust the agreement to their needs. We mainly work in a time & material model, which means that you are charged for a particular number of hours worked by your team members per billing period (e.g., monthly or daily). Still, some of our services, like Product Design, UX Research, UI Design, and others are estimated on the project basis. We’d love to share more details face-to-face and discuss your preferences.

What if I want to keep some of the work in-house?

Totally up to you. We work based on the agreed scope and requirements. By all means, keep your existing team if it works for you. Your external team will simply complement what you’re already doing while bringing in a fresh perspective and greater scalability.
